  • An archive manager that handles a wide range of archives quickly and easily.

    -  Opens a wide range of archive formats, including ZIP, RAR, 7Z, EGG, ALZ, and ISO.
    -  Previews images and text without extracting the archive.
    -  Uses a dedicated ZIP engine for fast compression and extraction.
    -  Supports password protection, filename encryption, and split archives.
    -  Compresses and extracts files directly from the Windows Explorer context menu.
